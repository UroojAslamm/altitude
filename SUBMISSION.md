# ideaForge submission: Team Pythons

**App:** ASCEND by Altitude
**Live URL:** https://altitude-liart.vercel.app
**Repo:** https://github.com/UroojAslamm/altitude

## Try it

Open the live URL and tap **Log in**, then **Try as Student**. Use a second device or window for **Try as Barista**.

## Five talking points

1. **It works end to end.** Phone orders, the barista laptop updates live, the phone gets the ready alert, and meters land exactly once.
2. **Built around the real counter.** Sold-out switches, undo, unpaid badges, and preferences highlighted in brass. Students see who is making their order and can tip them.
3. **The climb.** Meters raise students from Base Camp to Peak, and squads earn together. Clubs race on a monthly leaderboard.
4. **Server-side rules.** Prices, discounts, order numbers, and meters run in the database. A tampered browser can't change them. Automated tests cover the golden path.
5. **Honest and cheap to roll out.** Free tiers, demo payment today, and built to plug into MOBIPOS. Seeded demo data is labeled as demo data.
